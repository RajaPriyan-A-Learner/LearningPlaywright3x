🌌 Null vs Undefined

`undefined` means a variable exists but hasn't been assigned a value. `null` is an intentional assignment of "no value".

🔢 Number Literals

JS numbers are always double-precision floats. You can also write numbers in Binary (0b), Hex (0x), and use Numeric Separators (_).

let million = 1_000_000;
let hex = 0xFF; // 255
